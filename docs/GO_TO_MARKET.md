# 30-day go-to-market validation

## Hypothesis

One DACH service-business segment will pay for an **Anfragen-Autopilot** that reduces missed enquiries and moves qualified requests into booking and follow-up.

This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed market fact.

## Focus

- ICP: one German service niche with 10–50 employees.
- Offer: enquiry capture + qualification + booking handoff.
- CTA: `Kostenlose Anfrage-Leak-Analyse`.
- One profile link: German landing page with booking, consent, privacy information and UTM tracking.

Do not sell “an AI chatbot”. Sell a measurable enquiry and appointment outcome.

## Channel mix

Primary:

- three educational Reels per week;
- referrals and local implementation partners;
- manual account research;
- opt-in landing page;
- selected B2B calls only after case-specific legal review.

Not approved:

- bulk cold email;
- scraped contact databases;
- automated Instagram DMs;
- paid traffic before one paid pilot and reliable funnel tracking.

## Weekly plan

### Week 1 — evidence

- interview 5 businesses in one niche;
- document exact missed-enquiry workflow;
- publish 3 Reels: problem, teardown, demo;
- build landing page CTA and booking tracking;
- research 15 fictional/test-safe records in tooling; keep real list private.

### Week 2 — conversations

- interview 5 more businesses;
- publish 3 Reels;
- ask partners/customers for warm introductions;
- run 3 tailored demos;
- record objections and language used by buyers.

### Week 3 — paid pilot

- publish 3 Reels using real objections;
- run 2 more demos;
- propose two paid, time-boxed pilots;
- define baseline, target metric, manual fallback and data boundary.

### Week 4 — decision

- publish 3 Reels;
- review profile visit → landing visit → booking → show → pilot conversion;
- produce case-study draft only from measured data;
- keep, change or stop ICP/offer.

## Experiment thresholds

Targets, not promises:

- 10 relevant interviews;
- 12 Reels;
- 50 manually researched companies;
- 15 commercial conversations;
- 5 demos;
- 2 paid pilots.

If repeated pain or willingness to pay is absent, change ICP/offer. Do not compensate with more traffic.

## Tracking

UTM example:

`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=anfrage_leak&utm_content=reel_01`

Weekly dashboard:

- qualified profile visits;
- landing-page conversion;
- booked and attended calls;
- demo-to-pilot conversion;
- pilot setup time and delivery cost;
- verified customer outcome.

## Legal boundary

German electronic marketing generally requires prior express consent under § 7 UWG, with a narrow existing-customer exception. B2B calls require a defensible case-specific basis for presumed consent. GDPR transparency, objection handling and deletion workflows still apply. Obtain German legal review before campaign launch.
