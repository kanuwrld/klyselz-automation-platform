# Prospect validation

Prospect score is prioritization, not permission to contact.

## Required record

Before `verified` state, manually confirm:

- legal business name and working public website;
- region and target-industry match;
- public source URL and verification date;
- real operational signal tied to KLYSELZ offer;
- relevant decision-maker role, without guessing private identity;
- approved contact channel and reason it is lawful;
- opt-out / do-not-contact state;
- data retention deadline.

Real records stay outside Git. Public repository contains fictional sample only.

## Lifecycle

`researched → verified → channel-approved → contacted → replied → qualified → pilot/customer`

Any objection moves record immediately to `do-not-contact`. Stale or unverifiable records move to `discarded`.

## Channel rules for Germany

- Email/DM: no bulk cold blast. Electronic marketing generally requires prior express consent; narrow existing-customer exception needs all statutory conditions.
- B2B phone: use only with documented, case-specific basis for presumed consent and legal review.
- Instagram: no scraping, bot login or automatic mass DM.
- Preferred: inbound request, referral, event conversation, partner introduction, or explicit opt-in landing page.

Human approval stays mandatory before any send or call.
