# KLYSELZ Logo System

## Concept

KLYSELZ identity uses a constructed **signal K**: a vertical stem, two precise diagonals, and one small dot. The dot is the system signal: incoming request, automation trigger, or active status.

## Primary Mark

Use `klyselz-lockup-black.svg` as default on white or off-white backgrounds. Use `klyselz-lockup-white.svg` on black backgrounds.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is uppercase, wide, calm, and technical without feeling cold. It should never be compressed.

Rules:

- Always `KLYSELZ`.
- No tagline attached to the core logo.
- Use generous spacing around the wordmark.
- Do not use the wordmark inside text-heavy paragraphs.

## Symbol

Use the symbol alone for:

- favicon;
- social avatar;
- app icon;
- small dashboard brand mark;
- watermark;
- slide section marker.

Do not use symbol alone for first-touch marketing unless the full brand name appears nearby.

## Clearspace

Use at least `1x` signal-dot diameter around symbol and `0.5x` symbol height around lockup.

## Minimum Sizes

| Asset | Digital | Print |
|---|---:|---:|
| Wordmark | 120px wide | 32mm |
| Lockup | 160px wide | 42mm |
| Symbol | 20px | 6mm |

## Backgrounds

Allowed:

- black on white;
- black on off-white;
- white on black;
- white on deep grey;
- black watermark at 4-8% opacity.

Not allowed:

- colour backgrounds;
- gradients;
- photos behind logo without strong contrast;
- textured backgrounds;
- shadows/glow.
