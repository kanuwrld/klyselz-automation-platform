# KLYSELZ Brand Kit

Minimal black-and-white system for website, dashboards, proposals, contracts, presentations, social media and client material.

## 1. Brand Core

**Name:** KLYSELZ
**Spelling:** use `KLYSELZ` in logo; `Klyselz` is allowed in prose. Never translate or transliterate.

**Category:** AI automation agency for local businesses.

**Short formula:**
KLYSELZ builds enquiry systems for local businesses: faster replies, structured lead capture, booking handoff and visible operations.

**EN one-liner:**
Enquiry systems that answer around the clock and help local businesses stop losing qualified requests.

**DE one-liner:**
KI-Systeme, die Kundenanfragen rund um die Uhr beantworten und lokale Betriebe vor verlorenen Anfragen schützen.

**Tagline primary DE:**
Nie wieder Kundenanfragen verlieren.

**Tagline secondary DE:**
KI, die für dein Geschäft antwortet.

**Tagline EN:**
Automation that responds while your team is busy.

## 2. Positioning

KLYSELZ does not position itself as “a bot” or “a website”. It presents an operating system around inbound enquiries:

- respond faster;
- lose fewer enquiries;
- move qualified people toward booking;
- show work in one dashboard;
- maintain the system every month.

**Market position:** calm, precise automation for small service businesses. No AI hype, “digital magic” or enterprise theatre.

## 3. Personality

**Quiet:** no visual noise.
**Sharp:** short language and clear claims.
**Useful:** every element explains value.
**Reliable:** looks credible enough to handle enquiries.
**Human:** plain language without technical theatre.

## 4. Visual Principle

Black, white, grey, space, grid and disciplined typography. Contrast, scale, spacing and exact wording create emphasis.

Use:

- white background + black text;
- black sections + white text;
- thin borders;
- restrained cards;
- uppercase wordmark;
- no gradients, no colour accents, no decorative blobs.

Avoid:

- blue/purple AI palette;
- neon;
- glossy 3D;
- stock robot imagery;
- rounded cartoon bot faces;
- fake “enterprise” seals;
- dense layouts without breathing room.

## 5. Logo System

Core idea: **signal K**. Symbol combines a constructed `K` with a small signal dot. It reads as:

- K for KLYSELZ;

## 7. Typography

**Primary UI/font:** Inter or system sans fallback.
**Fallback stack:** `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

**Logo wordmark:** uppercase, bold, expanded tracking.
**Headlines:** 700-800 weight, tight line-height, no negative letter spacing.
**Body:** 400-500 weight, clear and calm.
**Labels:** uppercase only for short navigation, badges, or metadata.

Scale:

- Display: 56/60 desktop, 40/44 mobile.
- H1: 48/52 desktop, 36/40 mobile.
- H2: 36/42 desktop, 28/34 mobile.
- Body: 16/26.
- Small: 14/20.
- Caption: 12/16.

## 8. Layout System

**Grid:** 12 columns desktop, 4 columns mobile.
**Max content width:** 1120px.
**Section padding:** 96px desktop, 64px mobile.
**Card radius:** 8px max.
**Button radius:** 6px.
**Border:** 1px solid `#E4E4E7`.
**Icon stroke:** 1.5px, black or grey only.

## 9. Voice

Tone: precise, direct, confident, never loud.

Say:

- “Keine Anfrage bleibt liegen.”
- “Antwortet auch nach Feierabend.”
- “Ein Dashboard für Anfragen, Termine und Ergebnisse.”
- “7 Tage testen, dann entscheiden.”

Avoid:

- “Revolutionäre KI-Lösung”
- “Next generation AI”
- “Wir transformieren Ihr Business”
- “Automatisierung der Zukunft”

## 11. Usage Rules

Minimum logo clearspace: height of symbol dot around all sides.
Minimum wordmark width: 120px digital, 32mm print.
Minimum symbol size: 20px digital, 6mm print.

Use black logo on light backgrounds. Use white logo on black backgrounds. Do not use grey logo except as watermark at low opacity.

Do not:

- stretch;
- rotate;
- add shadow;
- put logo in gradients;
- put logo inside pill buttons;
- recolour;
- change letter spacing;
- use different brand casing.

## 12. Production Note

SVGs use live text for wordmark variants so they remain editable. Before trademark registration, print production, or large paid campaigns, outline final typography and run trademark/domain checks.
